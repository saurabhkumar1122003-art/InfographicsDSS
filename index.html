01
The Hook
Mechanism
Reward System

What your brain thinks

"I'm saving
money."

A pleasant, mathematical certainty. You've spotted an edge.

What is actually happening

  • The word "FREE" pulls focus to the gift — not the spend.
  • You never asked: do I even need this?
  • You never asked: was I planning to buy this?
  • You never asked: am I spending more than I should?
Psychological trigger
Reward System Activation
Your brain releases a small hit of dopamine because it feels like you're winning the transaction — not paying for it.

Psychological trigger
Instant Reward Bias

The brain disproportionately prefers immediate pleasure over long-term savings. A drink in eight minutes feels more real than a hundred rupees next month.

"Not every desire
is truly yours."

Before you tap "Buy" — ask yourself

  • Do I actually need this?
  • Would I buy it without the offer?
  • Am I buying because of pressure, or because of emotion?
  • Will I remember this purchase tomorrow?
Control your impulses. Think before you spend. Save your money wisely.
